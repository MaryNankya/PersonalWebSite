Hello!

PhD program in Engineering and Applied Science, specializing in the Computer Science and Information Science track.

My research will sit at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity, with a focus on improving the reliability, security, and resilience of autonomous systems. This includes developing methods to (1) model and quantify risk, (2) detect and mitigate adversarial or anomalous behavior, and (3) enable robust recovery and graceful degradation under attacks, failures, or uncertain operating conditions. I’m particularly interested in ensuring AI-enabled autonomy remains verifiable, explainable, and operationally safe, even when facing sophisticated threat actors and evolving system complexity

I recently Served as an Adjunct Professor at Fitchburg State University, Computer Science Department. I also recently served as the Director of Data and Evaluation at Hearth Inc., Boston. I bring a rich background in both academia and industry and with recently published research in a wide range of topics, including cybersecurity, AI, machine learning, wireless communication, and next-generation technologies such as 5G, 6G, and beyond.
